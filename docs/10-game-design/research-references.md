## 1. Method and verification status
- **v0.3 change:** the official Takara Tomy, Hasbro and FURYU hosts were added to this repository's cloud-environment allowlist and the pages below were **downloaded and read directly** on 2026-11-21 (text extracted from the PDFs/HTML). - Pages still **not** read directly (hosts not allow-listed): Hasbro consumer-care pages, Apple App Store, Steam, Nintendo eShop, fan wikis, retailer blogs. Their entries are marked *search index only* or *secondary*.
- The latest official regulation found on the Takara Tomy Asia campaign page is **"BEYBLADE X regulation – Vol.11 (Asia ver.)"**; its PDF is image-based and could not be text-extracted, so the **6th edition (November 2024)** text was used. A person should confirm Vol.11 did not change the finish rules quoted below (action R-3).
- No proprietary formula exists in any official source read. Official sources describe mechanics **qualitatively**; every number in this project is an original design value.

Verification labels: **VERIFIED** = read directly in the official document · **INDEX** = seen only via search-engine extract · **SEC** = secondary source · **CORRECTED** = v0.2 statement found wrong.

## 2. Official Beyblade X mechanics (verified quotes)
### 2.1 Components
| Official part | Official description (quote) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Blade** | "The main part of the Bey that collide directly with the opponents. Performance varies depending on its shape and weight." | O-1, O-5 |
| **Ratchet** | "The middle-layer of the Bey that determine the Bey's movement, balance of the center of gravity and how the Bey collide." Product notes: 3-60 "set the Bey low. Adopts 3 blades"; 4-80 "set the Bey high. Use 4 blades". | O-1, O-5 |
| **Bit** | "The shaft tip part determines the movement of the Beyblade. Dash performance and burst resistance are depending on the type of bit." | O-1, O-5 |

### 2.3 Finishes, points and battle rules
| Official fact | Quote / content | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Points | Xtreme Finish **3**, Over Finish **2**, Burst Finish **2**, Spin Finish **1**; "The first to earn 4 points wins the match." | O-2 (Hasbro names: Knockout = 2, Survivor = 1, same values — O-4) |
| **Burst Finish** | "This occurs when the parts of the opponent's Bey detach and separate before your Bey. (such as the blade, ratchet, or bit)" | O-2 |
| **Spin Finish** | "This occurs when the spinning speed of the opponent's Bey in its original rotation direction becomes zero in the battle zone before your Bey." | O-2 |
| Over / Xtreme Finish | Opponent's Bey "completely enters the over zone / Xtreme zone and cannot return to the battle zone". | O-2 |
| Timing of finishes | Burst: "The moment a Bey's parts detach and separate." Spin: "The moment the Bey's spinning speed … becomes zero." First finish wins. | O-2 |
| **Simultaneous finishes** | "If … Xtreme Finish, Over Finish, Burst Finish, Spin Finish, or any combination of these occur simultaneously, it is considered a draw, and the battle will be restarted." | O-2 |
| Reverse | A Bey that returns to the battle zone "while maintaining its rotation" cancels the over/Xtreme finish. | O-2 |
| **3-player battle (official)** | "Start a 3-player battle. All points from the finishing moves of the other two players are awarded to the last person remaining. The player who first accumulates a total of 5 points wins the match." Alternative: "The last person remaining, regardless of the finishing move, earns one win. The first player to achieve two wins is the overall winner." | O-2 |
| Launch call | "Shoot with the call 'Three, Two, One, Go Shoot'." | O-2 |
| Deck format | 3 Beys; "A player cannot use the same part multiple times among the three Beys." | O-2 |

## 3. Official mechanics vs OUR original game system
| Area | Official Beyblade X | Our system (original, proposal unless marked [C]) | Relationship |
|---|---|---|---|
| Parts | 3 parts: Blade, Ratchet, Bit (O-1) | 4 slots: RING, CORE, TIP, SPIRIT | *Inspired*: RING≈Blade (contact, weight), CORE≈Ratchet (height, CoG, lock), TIP≈Bit (movement, dash, burst resistance). **SPIRIT has no official equivalent** (original; carries Magic + skill). Names original. |
| Attributes | **None** — official sources give no numeric stats | ATK, DEF, STA, MAG 0–100 [C] | Original. "Magic" has no official counterpart. |
| Energy | None | Derived power value [C concept] | Original |
| **Type determination** | **Determined by the Bit type** (O-1) | Computed from ATK/DEF/STA distribution; subtype from part properties | **Different by design.** Option B (closer to official): the TIP part's designed type decides the type. See CLS spec §2 and OQ-ST-07. |
| Type matchups | Attack > Stamina > Defense > Attack as "compatibility", Balance combines | Emerges from attributes/properties; measured, not hard-coded | Inspired (tendency) |
| Burst Finish | Parts detach and separate (O-2); burst resistance depends on the bit (O-1); strong shoot can engage burst stopper (O-3) | Lock Integrity gauge, spin-dependent threshold, cumulative damage | **Mechanism original.** Official sources give no damage model. Cumulative damage is our design choice (see BURST spec §2). |
| Spin Finish | Spin "becomes zero" (O-2) | Spin < 8 RPS (stop threshold) | Adapted (game threshold instead of exactly zero) |
| Over / Xtreme | Pocket / zone entry, cannot return (O-2) | OVER = leave arena / pocket ≥ 0.5 s; XTREME optional zone | Adapted |
| Simultaneous finish | Draw and battle restart (O-2) | Proposal: shared placement (no restart in online FFA) | **Deviation**, needs decision (OQ-GP-04) |
| Points | 3/2/2/1, first to 4 (O-2) | MVP: placement only; finish type recorded | Deviation (FFA elimination) |
| Multi-player | Official 3-player battle formats (O-2) | Up to 5 competitors FFA [C] | Official precedent for >2 players; 5 is our choice |
| Launch | Physical launcher; shoot strength measured by Beylogger/Battlepass (O-3) | 5-second rapid press → RPS [C] | Digital analogue; curve original |
| X Dash | Rail engages bit gear → super acceleration (O-1) | Dash rail in arena + "Rail Dash" skill | Inspired |
| Gauges / commands | XONE: Survivor, Burst (staged), Xtreme, Active gauges; Active Move command (O-5) | Spin meter, staged Burst gauge, Skill gauge, Skill button [C button] | Inspired (closest official precedent for our skill button) |
| Spin direction | Left/right/dual; low-speed burst and spin absorption vs opposite spin (O-3) | Field exists; no MVP effect | Post-MVP candidate, now with **official** evidence (upgraded from secondary) |

## 5. Corrections made in v0.3
1. **Type determination:** official Beyblade X type follows the Bit (O-1). Our distribution-based classification is now labelled *original*, and an official-style alternative is offered (OQ-ST-07).
2. **R-08 removed** as an official basis for cumulative burst damage. The burst model's cumulative design is now justified as a game-design choice, with only these official anchors: parts detach (O-2), burst resistance depends on the bit (O-1), strong shoot protects (O-3).
3. **Simultaneous finishes:** official rule is draw + restart (O-2); our "shared placement" is a deviation for online FFA and needs a decision.
4. **Opposite spin:** effects are officially documented (O-3), so post-MVP spin-direction mechanics now have official grounding.
5. **Regulation URL** corrected; latest regulation edition (Vol.11) noted.
6. **Official precedent for >2 players** (3-player battle) added.

## 7. What is original (not taken from any source)
