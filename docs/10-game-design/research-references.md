## 1. Method & limitations (read first)
- Searches prioritised Takara Tomy, Hasbro, official game publishers (FURYU, Nintendo eShop, Steam store pages) and official tournament regulations, then secondary sources.
- **Limitation:** the official hosts (`takaratomyasia.com`, `beyblade.com`, `beyblade.takaratomy.co.jp`, `cs.furyu.jp`) **could not be fetched directly** from the design environment (DNS blocked by the network allowlist). Facts marked *"via search index"* come from search-engine extracts of those official pages, not a full read. **Before Gate G0 acceptance, a person should open each official URL and confirm the quoted fact** (column "Verify").
- No proprietary formula was found or copied. Official sources describe mechanics **qualitatively**; every number in this project is an original design value.

Classification: **OFF** = official manufacturer/publisher/regulation · **SEC** = secondary (retailer, fan wiki, community rulebook, press) · **INT** = our interpretation.

## 4. What is original (not taken from any source)
Attribute scales (0–100), the Energy formula, the launch curve, the RPS-influence weighting function, all collision/burst equations and coefficients, skill catalogue, part names (RING/CORE/TIP/SPIRIT), "Magic" as an attribute. No character names, product names, logos or art from the Beyblade franchise are used in the game design.
