# Product Vision

| Document ID | DOC-VIS-001 | Status | Draft — content is [C] unless labelled |
|---|---|---|---|

## 1. Vision statement
A cross-platform, online, 3D spinning-top battle game in which players build a persistent identity —
their tops, their team, their rank and their match history — and prove their skill in short, fair
matches of **up to five competitors**, on desktop or mobile, with one account.

## 2. Target players [A]
Fans of spinning-top battle games and casual/competitive arena players on phones and PCs.
Age rating, regions and languages are **[TBD]** (OQ-PL-05, OQ-AC-09).

## 3. Product goals (Brief §B2) [C]
| ID | Goal |
|---|---|
| PG-1 | Enjoyable, skill-influenced spinning-top battles |
| PG-2 | Small fair matches, max 5 active competitors |
| PG-3 | ~100 concurrent connected players at peak (planning target) |
| PG-4 | Persistent accounts, profiles, teams, ranks, inventories, match records |
| PG-5 | Meaningful social and competitive identity (not just a chat room) |
| PG-6 | Mobile and desktop clients in one ecosystem |
| PG-7 | Maintainable by a future team |
| PG-8 | Cost-conscious hosting that grows with measured demand |

## 4. Non-goals (Brief §B3) [C]
- No 100-player arenas; no battle with more than 5 competitors.
- No assumption of a constant 100-player population.
- No direct client access to MySQL; no client-authoritative results.
- No real-money betting/gambling in MVP. No voice top-control in MVP.
- No requirement for a huge open world in the first release.

## 5. Product pillars [P] (v0.2: see GDD §3)
1. **Launch & clash** — rapid-press launch is skill expression; steering and skills shape the clash; Burst Finish is the climax.
2. **My top, my identity** — loadouts, records and team badges are visible everywhere.
3. **Play anywhere** — same account, same rules, same results on every platform.
4. **Fair by construction** — the server decides; clients only ask and display.

## 6. Long-term vision (not MVP) [C concept]
Bounded first-person exploration with chests and in-world challenges ("the arena rises from the
ground"), team battles, tournaments run by approved organizers, proximity voice, voice commands,
and — only if separately approved — virtual-item stakes.
